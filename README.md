## Why Google Pay isn't in your payment list

OKX names three causes: no valid Visa or Mastercard saved in Google Wallet, an unsupported device, or an account that hasn't finished verification. Three more follow directly from the conditions OKX attaches to the feature.

1. **No card in Google Wallet.** The most common one. Add the card in Google Wallet, then reopen the payment screen.
2. **Unsupported device.** OKX states plainly that Google Pay isn't supported on iOS devices at this time, so the option never renders on an iPhone.
3. **Verification not completed.** Card payments stay locked until your OKX account clears verification.
4. **The card isn't US-issued.** A perfectly valid Visa from another country won't trigger the button.
5. **It's a credit card.** OKX lists debit cards only for this route. Several banks also classify crypto purchases on credit as cash advances, which is one reason issuers are cautious about them.
6. **The name doesn't match.** The cardholder name in Google Wallet has to be the legal name on your OKX account.

## Declined payment, money still missing

Card issuers can decline any transaction at their own discretion, and that decision has nothing to do with your OKX account standing. When a decline happens at the payment attempt stage, your bank may place a temporary hold on the amount even though nothing was bought.

Those funds are usually released automatically within a few days, depending on your bank's processing time. If the hold is still there after a week, contact your bank; OKX can't release a hold the bank placed. A better first move is to buy with a debit card, since that avoids the cash-advance treatment some issuers apply to credit.

The practical split: an exchange account like OKX is a trading balance that comes with withdrawal rules and identity verification, while a wallet purchase puts the coins under your own keys immediately. ## FAQ

**Does OKX's Google Pay option work on an iPhone?**
No. Google Pay isn't supported on iOS devices on OKX, so the payment method won't appear.

**Can I use a credit card?**
Not through this route. OKX lists US-issued Visa and Mastercard debit cards as the payment method for Google Pay purchases.

**Can I send funds back to Google Pay?**
No. Withdrawals to digital wallets such as Google Pay or Apple Pay aren't supported. Withdraw to your linked bank account.

**How soon can I withdraw crypto bought with Google Pay?**
There may be a withdrawal hold for protection after a Google Pay purchase, and OKX doesn't quote a fixed length for it on the help page. Assume a delay rather than instant access.

**My payment was declined but my bank shows a deduction. How long until it's back?**
The bank's temporary hold usually clears automatically within a few days. If a week passes without release, call your bank.
